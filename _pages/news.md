## MPM Simulation of Submarine Landslides

**Period:** 2024 - Present  
**Role:** Researcher

This project aims to develop a numerical framework for simulating submarine landslides using the Material Point Method.

### Objectives

- Develop 2D MPM models for submarine slope failure.
- Extend the model to 3D landslide runout simulation.
- Investigate the influence of soil strength, slope geometry, and seabed topography.
- Visualize velocity fields, displacement, runout distance, and deposition morphology.
